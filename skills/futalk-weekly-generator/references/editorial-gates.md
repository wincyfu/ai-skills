# Editorial gates

## Sources and links

- A discovery page helps find an item; it is not automatically a valid final URL.
- Prefer direct original articles, official tool entry points, and official event or competition pages.
- Record publication time for time-sensitive articles and closing time for active events.
- Keep owner-supplied links distinguishable from independently discovered candidates.
- Record explicit rejection reasons for newer candidates that were reviewed but not selected.

## Sections

A typical issue may include AI news, design or product practice, useful tools, and active competitions. Section names and target counts come from private rules. Quality takes priority over count.

- News must describe a concrete event and use credible reporting rather than promotional copy.
- Practice items must contain a reusable process, case, method, or judgment—not merely announce a product.
- Tool items must link to a usable product, repository, plugin, template, or documentation entry.
- Competition items must still be open and link to an official detail, rules, registration, or submission page.

## Images

- Prefer article images, official cover/share images, clean first-screen captures, or video frames.
- Use horizontal images suitable for the target publication.
- Reject login/captcha pages, blocked pages, cookie or newsletter overlays, blank ad regions, unloaded captures, desktop/window chrome, misleading portraits, and decorative images without a recognizable subject.
- Record the image path, provenance type, dimensions, source URL, and review result.
- Generated images are a fallback only when source-bound images are unavailable or unsuitable.
## Copy

- Summaries describe the source content itself, not why the editor selected it.
- Avoid fabricated claims, reader advice disguised as summary, and source attribution inserted into the prose unless required by private rules.
- Preserve Chinese titles. Translate non-Chinese titles faithfully while retaining the original title in the audit.
